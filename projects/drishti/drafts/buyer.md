### Is it worth the money and the risk, and will it make me look good?

#### The headline

Real PPE compliance, measured on camera across every shift, rose from 69% to 91% in an eight-week pilot and met the signed target [M:K1_base] [M:K1_result] [E:c1].
Three months later the pilot areas held at 94%, while areas added later were at 87% and climbing, as the pilot areas once were [M:K1_post_pilot] [M:K1_post_scale] [E:e64].
Restricted-zone entries are down 77% since the client installed guards where the camera data showed people crossing [M:K2_post_change] [E:e57].

#### Money, only where it was measured

The client's finance team claimed money only where it could measure it: at the truck gate [E:e43b] [E:e71].
Truck turnaround fell 24% on like-for-like camera data [M:gate_like_for_like].
Most of that drop came after the client's logistics team started dispatch slots run from the system's queue view; a separate client investment in gate infrastructure also helped [E:e59] [M:gate_step_slots] [M:gate_step_infra].
When finance asked how much was the vendor's, the vendor said: "Honestly, we can't split it cleanly." [E:e65]
Finance replied: "Good. That's an answer I can defend." [E:e66]
Transporter detention charges fell 68% from the June-July average to September; that is one month, and finance credits the project with only a share of it [M:det_change_pct] [E:e71].
On the client's own conservative view, payback is a little over two years [E:e72].
The client did not count injuries avoided, and neither do we [E:e74] [E:e67].

#### What the sponsor actually bought

The unit head had bought dashboards before that nobody opened [E:e05].
The promise was different: "We'd measure response time, not logins." [E:e05b]
Alerts on the pilot cameras are now acknowledged in about 2 minutes [M:K3_post_pilot].

#### The risk, honestly

The project had real setbacks and each was handled in the open: a 41-hour camera outage, a privacy stop on face recognition, and two pilot targets missed before being fixed [E:e36] [E:e34] [E:e48].
The signed requirements lowered the vendor's own proposed targets before work began, so the results are measured against numbers the client chose [E:e13].

It went from a 12-camera proof of concept to 40 pilot cameras to plant-wide use on existing cameras in about five months [E:e16] [E:e31] [E:e60].
